Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the “Pinterest Poster App” (the “App”), a personal automation tool that schedules and posts Pins to Pinterest using the official Pinterest API.

2. Information we collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users.

When you visit this privacy policy page hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically collect certain technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in accordance with GitHub’s own privacy practices. This data is not controlled by us.

3. How we use information

We do not use any personal data because the App does not collect any. The App only interacts with Pinterest’s API using OAuth tokens that are stored securely in AWS Secrets Manager for backend automation.

4. Third‑party services

  • GitHub Pages: hosts this privacy policy page. See GitHub’s privacy policy for details on their data handling.
  • Pinterest API: used by the App to create Pins. Pinterest’s own privacy policy and terms apply to any data processed via their platform.
  • AWS (Secrets Manager, Lambda, S3, EventBridge): used to run the scheduling and posting logic. AWS’s privacy policy applies to any infrastructure logs they may retain.

5. Data security

OAuth tokens and credentials are stored in AWS Secrets Manager and are not exposed in client‑side code or public repositories.

6. Your rights

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is no user data for us to provide, correct, or delete. For questions about how Pinterest handles your data, please refer to Pinterest’s privacy policy and settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made.

8. Contact

For any privacy‑related questions, you can contact us at: learn-thirukkural@aanandtamizh.com.
